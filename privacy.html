Privacy

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and ships no advertising pixels.

What is stored on your device

Nothing persistent. The page holds its GitHub counters in memory for the length of your visit and clears them when the tab closes. No local storage, no session storage, no identifiers.

Third party requests

The page loads Geist from Google Fonts and public profile counts from the GitHub API. Those services receive your IP address and user agent as part of a normal request, and their own privacy policies apply. Nothing else leaves your browser.

No accounts, no forms

There is no contact form and no login. JARVIS runs entirely in your browser against a local knowledge base; questions are never transmitted. Every contact link opens an external service, and that service handles what you send.
