Privacy

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and ships no advertising pixels.

What is stored on your device

Almost nothing. One session-storage flag (plSeen) keeps the intro animation from replaying on every visit; the browser clears it when the tab closes. No local storage, no identifiers, no profiles.

Third party requests

The page loads Outfit and JetBrains Mono from Google Fonts, Three.js from a public CDN, and public repository star counts from the GitHub API. Those services receive your IP address and user agent as part of a normal request, and their own privacy policies apply. Nothing else leaves your browser.

No accounts, no forms

There is no contact form and no login. KAREN runs entirely in your browser against a local knowledge base; questions are never transmitted. Every contact link opens an external service, and that service handles what you send.
