Privacy

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and ships no advertising pixels.

What is stored on your device

One session storage entry caches your public GitHub counters for the length of your visit. It holds repository, star and follower counts only. Closing the tab clears it.

Third party requests

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, animation and 3D libraries from the esm.sh content delivery network, and public profile counts from the GitHub API. Those services receive your IP address and user agent as part of a normal request. Their own privacy policies apply.

No accounts, no forms

There is no contact form and no login. Every contact link opens an external service, and that service handles what you send.
