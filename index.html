01 / Profile

Three and a half years inside the perimeter.

I run web, Android, iOS and API penetration tests across the full VAPT lifecycle: scoping, reconnaissance, exploitation, lateral movement simulation and remediation reporting.

My engagements sit mostly in BFSI and telecom, mapped to OWASP Top 10, PTES and CERT-In's Secure Gating Process. I have found everything from textbook injection flaws to logic bugs and AI prompt injection through obfuscation in production systems, and I ship a reproducible proof of concept with each one.

Outside client work I write public research through #Learn365, publish on Medium, and record security walkthroughs on YouTube.
